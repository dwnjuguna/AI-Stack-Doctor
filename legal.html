DATA PROCESSING
How client intake data is processed — relevant for enterprise and consulting use.
⚠ For Consultants: If you submit client data through the intake form on behalf of a client organization, you are responsible for ensuring you have the appropriate authorization and legal basis to share that data. You should inform your clients that their technology stack information will be processed by this service.
Data Controller
For the purposes of GDPR and similar regulations, the operator of AI Stack Doctor (the GitHub repository owner) acts as the Data Controller for any personal data submitted through the hosted service.
Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)
  • Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — processing intake data to generate the requested audit report
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — where you explicitly confirm submission of your data
  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — where a paid service agreement exists (future paid tier)
What Data Is Processed
  • Company name, industry, size, and location
  • AI tools and platforms declared in the intake form
  • Contact name and email (if provided)
  • Maturity self-assessment and audit goals
This data is processed by the Anthropic Claude API to generate the audit report. Anthropic's data processing terms apply. Company names may be used as search queries through your configured search engine.
International Transfers
The hosted service runs on Render.com infrastructure (United States). If you are an EU/UK user, data processed through this service is transferred to the US. Anthropic (AI processing) is also US-based. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable.
For EU enterprise users with strict data residency requirements: We recommend deploying the self-hosted version using the Private Server Mode, which keeps all data within your own infrastructure.
Data Minimisation
We collect only the information necessary to generate your audit. You are never required to submit revenue figures, budget details, or contact information — these are optional fields. The audit will function with only company name, industry, and size.

AI & AUTOMATED PROCESSING
Disclosure of AI use in accordance with EU AI Act and international best practices.
How AI Is Used
AI Stack Doctor uses Anthropic's Claude large language model to:
  • Research and synthesize publicly available information about company AI stacks
  • Score companies across 7 AI infrastructure domains
  • Generate strategic recommendations based on identified gaps
  • Produce peer benchmarking comparisons
AI Limitations and Accuracy
⚠ Important Limitations: AI-generated audit reports may contain inaccuracies, hallucinations, or outdated information. Scores reflect the AI model's analysis of publicly available data at the time of the audit and should not be treated as authoritative assessments. Always verify significant findings independently.
  • Reports are generated by AI and have not been verified by human experts
  • Company stack information may be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate
  • Compliance assessments are indicative only — not legal advice
  • Confidence scores ([H/M/L]) indicate data quality, not certainty
EU AI Act Compliance
AI Stack Doctor is classified as a General Purpose AI application used for informational and analytical purposes. It does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on individuals. It is not used in high-risk domains as defined in the EU AI Act Annex III (employment decisions, credit scoring, law enforcement, etc.).
Users retain full control over how they use audit outputs. No automated decisions are made without human review.
Right to Human Review
You always have the right to request human review of any AI-generated output. Contact us if you believe an audit report contains significant errors or has caused harm.
