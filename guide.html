// FAQ

QUESTIONS
ANSWERED.

Everything you need to know before you start your first audit.

Do I need to be technical to use this? ▼
Not at all. The intake form uses plain English questions — no code, no configuration, no setup required. If you know what tools your company uses for AI (even just "we use ChatGPT"), you have enough to get started. The report is also written in plain English, not engineer-speak.
Is my company data private and secure? ▼
Yes. The information you submit in the intake form is used only to generate your audit report. We do not sell your data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising. Company names may be used as search queries through DuckDuckGo (privacy-respecting). Full details in our Privacy Policy. For maximum privacy, self-host the tool using Private Server Mode.
How accurate are the audit scores? ▼
Scores for well-documented companies (Meta, Google, Stripe, etc.) are highly accurate — we have pre-loaded intelligence profiles confirmed from public sources. For less-documented companies, accuracy depends on public footprint. Every finding is marked with a confidence level: [H] High, [M] Medium, [L] Low. We always note uncertainty rather than fabricate. Intake form data is treated as ground truth and overrides web research.
How long does an audit take? ▼
The intake form takes 5–10 minutes depending on your role. The AI analysis takes about 90 seconds to run. You'll have a complete report with scores, ROI estimates, competitor benchmarks, and action plan ready in under 15 minutes total.
Is this really free? ▼
Yes — completely free and open source. The full codebase is available on GitHub under the MIT license. The hosted version at ai-stack-doctor.onrender.com is free to use. You do need your own Anthropic API key to run audits locally — API calls cost a small amount per audit (typically under $0.50). A future paid tier with additional features is planned.
Can I use this for client work? ▼
Absolutely — the Consultant intake form is specifically designed for this. Send your client the intake link before your first call, have them fill it in (10 minutes), and you arrive with a complete AI stack analysis ready. The PDF export is board-ready and client-presentable. Just ensure your client understands that scores are analytical estimates from public data, not verified assessments. See our Terms of Service for consultant usage terms.
What AI domains does it cover? ▼
Seven domains, each scored out of 14 points (Cloud AI Services out of 16): GenAI / LLMs — foundation models and generative AI adoption; Agentic AI — autonomous agents and orchestration; Machine Learning — training frameworks and model lifecycle; Data Engineering — pipelines, warehouses, and feature stores; AI Platforms — internal ML platforms and serving infrastructure; MLOps / LLMOps — monitoring, observability, and CI/CD for models; Cloud AI Services — AWS, GCP, and Azure AI services maturity.
